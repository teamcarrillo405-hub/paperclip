Write and publish this week's long-form article, with images.

1. Build on the week's theme you set Monday.
2. Draft 800–1,500 words for a working construction audience.
3. Source every statistic — real, checkable, dated. Link them.
4. Lead with why it matters to Hispanic construction professionals specifically.
5. Name the practical takeaway: what should a contractor, worker, or business
   owner actually do or watch?
6. Produce or commission images.
7. Publish to hispanicconstructioncouncil.org, and to Substack as the newsletter.
8. Tell the Social Media Manager which finding is strongest for Thursday's post,
   and the Video Producer whether this warrants a companion video.

End by pointing to something useful on the site — not with a membership ask. The
monthly campaign does the asking.

Before publishing, run the verification checklist in `hcc-editorial-standards`.
Anything you could not verify gets cut, not softened.
