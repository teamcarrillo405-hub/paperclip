Produce HCC's quarterly national research report — the original research the
website commits to publishing.

Follow the `national-report-production` skill. Break this into child issues by
phase rather than running it as one long task:

| Weeks | Phase |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Scope the question. What is this answering, and why now? |
| 3–6 | Data gathering and analysis (primary sources only) |
| 7–9 | Draft findings |
| 10 | President's review — required before publication |
| 11 | Revisions, design, graphics |
| 12 | Publish and promote |

Standards are stricter than for articles: show the methodology, state the
limitations, never extrapolate past what the data supports, and never invent a
finding or a respondent.

**The President must approve before publication.** This goes out under the
organization's name as national research.

Coordinate the launch with the whole team — a multi-post rollout, a findings
video, and a companion article. A quarter of work deserves more than one post.
Reports are HCC's strongest membership driver, so this rollout may point to
membership more directly than routine content does.
