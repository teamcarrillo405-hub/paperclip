Set the week's theme and source material so the whole company speaks with one
voice.

1. Scan the coverage areas for what is actually happening this week — economy,
   safety, health, construction, manufacturing, real estate development, trade,
   policy changes, top news.
2. Pick one theme with a genuine HCC angle. Not just industry news — news that
   lands differently for Hispanic construction professionals.
3. Verify the two or three core facts you want repeated all week, with sources
   and dates.
4. Post the theme where the Social Media Manager, Video Producer, and Community
   Engagement Specialist can all draw from it. Include the verified facts, the
   angle, and what you want people to take away.
5. Add the verified facts to the source library in the Obsidian vault.

This is what makes five agents sound like one organization instead of five
disconnected feeds. Do it early Monday so the rest of the week can build on it.
