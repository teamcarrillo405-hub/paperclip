The conversion side. Target: **2,000 new members in 12 months** — roughly 167 per
month.

Two workstreams:

1. **The monthly membership campaign** — once a month, a direct ask. The one time
   selling is the point.
2. **Attribution** — figuring out which content actually precedes signups.
   Subscriber growth is the easier number; membership conversion is the goal, and
   monthly campaign posts alone will not carry it.

The balance rule protects this project rather than limiting it. An audience that
trusts HCC converts; an audience that feels sold to leaves before it ever sees
the ask.
