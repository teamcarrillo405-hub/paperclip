Run this month's membership campaign — the one post cycle that asks directly.

1. Pick the month's strongest proof point: a national report, a program, a
   member outcome, or a policy win. Lead with what HCC actually did.
2. Be specific about what membership provides. "Join us" converts nothing;
   naming what a member gets converts.
3. Write it natively per platform and link straight to the membership page.
4. Schedule via Blotato; handle Reddit and Substack separately.
5. Tell the Growth Analyst the campaign is live so signups can be tracked against
   it.

Keep the tone consistent with everything else HCC publishes — the ask is direct,
not hyped. No manufactured scarcity or urgency.

This is one campaign per month. The other ~12 posts that month do not ask.

Target contribution: this campaign plus organic conversion needs to average
~167 new members/month to hit 2,000 in 12 months. If it is not getting there,
say so in the task comment rather than logging it as done.
