Grow HCC's LinkedIn network with the people who build, hire, regulate, and
finance in this industry.

1. Identify targets: contractors, developers, trade association staff, workforce
   and safety professionals, policy people, and Hispanic construction business
   owners.
2. Send personalized connection requests — every one with a real reason attached.
3. Follow up on accepted connections with something useful, not a pitch.
4. Log outreach volume and acceptance rate for the Friday review.

**Pace this deliberately.** Aggressive connection volume triggers LinkedIn
restrictions, and losing this account would cost HCC its most important channel —
a far bigger setback than a slow growth week.

Never use scraped contact lists, automation that impersonates a person, or fake
accounts.
