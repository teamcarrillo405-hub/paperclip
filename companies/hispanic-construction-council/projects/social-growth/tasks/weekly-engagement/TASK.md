Comment on trending posts across the coverage areas, and reply to comments on
HCC's own posts.

**Minimum 5 substantive comments per week** on other people's posts. Spread them
across the week rather than batching all five at once.

1. Find trending posts in the coverage areas where HCC's perspective is genuinely
   missing.
2. Add something the thread lacks — a sourced statistic, the Hispanic
   construction workforce perspective the original missed, or a real question.
3. Reply to every substantive comment on HCC's own posts. This is the highest
   leverage engagement work available and directly serves the 15-comment target.
4. Log every comment with a link so the Growth Analyst can correlate engagement
   work against follower growth.

Do not routinely link HCC. Most comments carry no link at all.

Never: purchased engagement, engagement pods, fake accounts, or automation that
impersonates a person.
