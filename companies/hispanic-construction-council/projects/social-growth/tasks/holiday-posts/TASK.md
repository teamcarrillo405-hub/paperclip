Plan and queue posts for upcoming holidays. Run this weekly, looking one to two
weeks ahead, so holiday content is scheduled before the day arrives.

Cover:

- **Federal holidays** — New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day,
  Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus/Indigenous Peoples' Day,
  Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas
- **Cultural observances that matter to this audience** — Hispanic Heritage Month
  (Sept 15–Oct 15), Día de los Muertos, Cinco de Mayo, Three Kings Day
- **Industry-meaningful days** — Labor Day and Workers' Memorial Day (April 28)
  carry real weight for a construction workforce; treat them with substance
  rather than a generic greeting

Guidance:

- Labor Day and Workers' Memorial Day are opportunities to say something real
  about construction work and worker safety, not just to post a greeting.
- Hispanic Heritage Month is a month, not a day — plan a series.
- Keep holiday posts warm and human. This is where "personable" matters most.
- No membership asks on holiday posts.

Confirm each is scheduled, and note any pending manual steps for Reddit and
Substack.
