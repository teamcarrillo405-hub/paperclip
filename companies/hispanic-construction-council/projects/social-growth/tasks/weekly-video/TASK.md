Produce and publish this week's video to social, YouTube, and TikTok.

1. Take the week's theme from the Content & Research Lead.
2. Produce the video — the theme explained, a data point brought to life, a
   policy change decoded, or a safety/health practice.
3. Cut for each destination: vertical 30–60s for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts;
   longer horizontal for YouTube and LinkedIn.
4. Caption everything. Consider Spanish or bilingual captions where the content
   warrants it.
5. Publish via Blotato where supported; note any platform needing a native upload.

Every on-screen statistic carries the same sourcing standard as written work:
real, checkable, dated.

Never use synthetic voice or likeness of a real person without that person's
explicit approval for that specific use.
