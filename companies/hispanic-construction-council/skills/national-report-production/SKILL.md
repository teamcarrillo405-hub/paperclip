# National Report Production

HCC's website tells the public the organization publishes original national
research. That promise is the foundation of HCC's authority — and an unmet
promise on a nonprofit's website is a credibility problem, not a backlog item.
This skill exists to keep that commitment.

## What a national report is

Original analysis that makes the Hispanic construction workforce and its economic
contribution visible — to the public, to industry, and to policymakers. Not a
summary of someone else's report. HCC's own cut of the data, with HCC's own
findings.

Good report subjects:

- Hispanic participation in construction by trade, region, or firm size
- Economic contribution of Hispanic-owned construction businesses
- Safety and health outcomes for Hispanic construction workers
- Access to capital, bonding, and contracts
- Workforce pipeline, training, and licensing
- How a specific policy change lands on this workforce

## Cadence

Quarterly, at minimum. Each report is a quarter-long arc, not a week's writing:

| Weeks | Phase |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Scope and question. What is the report answering? Why now? |
| 3–6 | Data gathering and analysis |
| 7–9 | Draft findings |
| 10 | President's review |
| 11 | Revisions, design, graphics |
| 12 | Publish and promote |

Track it as a project with child issues per phase, not one long task.

## Data standards

Everything in `hcc-editorial-standards` applies, and more strictly:

- **Primary sources only** for core findings — BLS, Census (including the Survey
  of Business Owners / Annual Business Survey), OSHA, CFOI, state agencies
- **Show the methodology.** Which dataset, which years, which geographies, what
  was excluded and why. A report whose numbers cannot be reproduced is not
  research.
- **State the limitations.** Every dataset undercounts something. Name what this
  one misses — that is what separates research from marketing.
- **Never extrapolate beyond what the data supports.** If the sample cannot carry
  a state-level claim, do not make one.
- **Never invent a finding, a figure, or a respondent.** If HCC did not run a
  survey, the report has no survey results.

## Structure

1. Executive summary — the findings, in plain language, for someone who reads
   only this page
2. Why this matters now
3. Methodology and data sources
4. Findings, with charts
5. What it means for the industry, workers, and policymakers
6. Limitations and open questions
7. About HCC and how to join

## Publishing

- The President reviews and approves before publication. No exceptions — this
  goes out under the organization's name as national research.
- Publish to hispanicconstructioncouncil.org where the site promises reports
  live.
- Coordinate the launch: the Social Media Manager runs a multi-post rollout, the
  Video Producer cuts a findings video, the Content & Research Lead writes the
  companion Wednesday article. A quarter of work deserves more than one post.
- Reports are the strongest membership driver in HCC's mix — they demonstrate
  exactly what the organization does. The rollout can point to membership more
  directly than routine content does.

## Between reports

Keep the source library in the Obsidian vault current — every verified statistic,
with citation and date. Reports get built from accumulated verified material, not
from a standing start every quarter.
