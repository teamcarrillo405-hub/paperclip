Set the week's direction.

1. Read the Growth Analyst's Friday review first. Start from what the data said,
   not from what you planned last week.
2. Confirm the week's theme with the Content & Research Lead.
3. Check for holidays this week or next and confirm they are queued.
4. Confirm each specialist knows what they own this week.
5. Post the plan as a comment where every agent can see it.
6. Check the goal math: are the 100,000-subscriber and 2,000-member trajectories
   on pace? Note honestly if they are not.

Once a month, report both goal numbers to the President — including when they are
behind. A miss reported early is still fixable.

Escalate to the President: any new policy position, any national report before
publication, anything naming an individual or partner organization, budget
pressure, or a goal materially off pace.
