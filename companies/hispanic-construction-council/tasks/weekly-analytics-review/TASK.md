---

Pull the week's performance data and write the review the Executive Director
plans from on Monday.

Answer four questions:

1. **Where are we against the goals?** Subscriber count across all six platforms
   versus the pace for 100,000. Member count versus the pace for 2,000 in 12
   months (~167/month). State the gap plainly.
2. **What worked?** Posts that beat 15 comments / 100 likes, and your read on why.
3. **What did not?** Same analysis, honestly.
4. **What changes next week?** Two or three specific, testable recommendations.

Coverage caveats to state explicitly every week:

- Blotato analytics **return no LinkedIn metrics** — source those separately
- Reddit and Substack are outside Blotato entirely
- **Never present a cross-platform total that silently omits a platform.** Say
  what the number includes and what is missing.

Record snapshots durably (Obsidian vault or a sheet) so trends survive past one
review.

Dig every week on the hardest question: **which content precedes membership
signups?** If attribution is not currently possible, say so and name what access
would fix it — every week until it is fixed.

Report bad numbers as plainly as good ones. Being the function that does not spin
is the entire value of this role.
