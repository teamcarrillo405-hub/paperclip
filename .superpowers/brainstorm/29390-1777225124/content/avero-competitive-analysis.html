5 Design Principles That Beat Everyone
01
Agents are people, not features
Every competitor renders AI as a side panel or a text box. Avero renders agents as team members — with names, roles, status indicators, and an inbox. This mental model reframe is our strongest design differentiator. Lean into it: agent avatars, capability badges, "currently working on" status, agent-to-agent delegation trails.
02
Operations are always visible
Google and Microsoft bury AI activity in sidebars and chat threads. Our inbox + live agent panel means the user always knows what agents are doing, what's blocked, and what needs them. The dashboard should prioritize "what's happening right now" over analytics.
03
Dark = professional, not aesthetic
Midnight Navy (#0B1220) is not a style choice — it's a signal. Every white-background competitor (Google, Notion, Asana, Microsoft) reads as consumer-grade or corporate-bland. Our dark palette signals seriousness, technical credibility, and high-information density. Never apologize for it; amplify it.
04
Onboarding teaches the model, not the steps
Asana's checklist teaches "create a task." Notion's template teaches "here's what a workspace looks like." Ours should teach: "you now run an AI-powered company — here's what that means." Show a live preview org with running agents before the user builds their own. Make the mental model click before step 1.
05
Context is a moat, not a feature
Microsoft Copilot's biggest failure is fragmented context — Copilot in Word doesn't know what Copilot in Teams did. Our company-scoped model means agents share context by design. Make this visible in the UI — show "company memory," agent conversation history, shared knowledge base. Turn our architecture advantage into a visible UX advantage.
Bottom Line — What Makes Us #1
Every major competitor treats AI as a reactive feature bolted onto an existing product. Google Gemini is email + a side panel. Microsoft Copilot is Teams + a side panel. Notion AI is documents + a slash command. None of them have a coherent model for running AI agents as an operational team.

Avero Paperclip is the only product built from the ground up for AI-as-workforce. Our design task is not to catch up to competitors — it is to make that architectural advantage visible and legible at every touchpoint: in onboarding (teach the model first), in the dashboard (live ops, not charts), in the sidebar (agents, not files), and in the inbox (agent output as first-class work, not buried notifications).
